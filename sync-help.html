Shortcut not found

The Shortcuts app has no shortcut named Friends sync. Names must match exactly, including spaces and numbers.
Fix it in one of two ways
  1. Open the Shortcuts app, long-press your Friends shortcut, choose Rename and call it exactly Friends sync.
  2. Or open Friends from your Home Screen, go to Settings → Calendar sync, and type your shortcut's exact name in Your shortcut's name.
No Friends shortcut at all?

Open Friends from your Home Screen, go to Settings and tap Get the Friends Sync shortcut. Or watch the setup guide.

You can close this page and go back to Friends on your Home Screen.
